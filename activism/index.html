Some context

DR Congo · 1990s -

7M+ Displaced

120+ armed groups in the east

Situation
M23 (the 'March 23 Movement'), backed by Rwanda, took Goma and Bukavu in 2025. Since early 2026 both sides have used armed drones and heavy artillery in neighbourhoods.
Ebola outbreak in May.
Why?
The Rubaya mining area supplies ~15–30% of the world's coltan. Coltan - metallic ore refined into the element tantalum.
Tantalum can hold a high electrical charge in a small space, which is why it is used in virtually every modern phone and computer.
Why it's silent
It's been going on for thirty years and nearly impossible to boycott. The computer this project is being worked on (and likely any used to view it) requires coltan.
HRW Africa Center ACAPS

Gaza, Palestine · Oct 2023 –

73,231 killed

173,686 injured — recorded by the Gaza Ministry of Health, reported through UN Health Cluster

The situation
Ceasefire: 10 October 2025. Between then and 11 August 2026, 1,259 people were killed.
90%+ of schools damaged/destroyed.
Who saying what
Israel rejects UN commission from September 2025 stating Israel has committed genocide in Gaza. South Africa's case under the Genocide Convention is still open at the ICJ.
Why it's different
It's on the news & social media.
UNRWA UN COI Gisha

Sudan · April 2023 -

13.6M Displaced

The largest crisis of its kind.

Situation
A paramilitary force called the RSF (Rapid Support Forces) is fighting the national army.
Famine confirmed in Darfur. 33.7 million people (every 2/3 Sudanese people) need aid to get through the year.
Officially
In January 2025 the US State Department determined the RSF committed genocide. Not an "activist finding."
Why it's quiet
No Western troops, no oil angle, & reporters can't get in.
2026 aid appeal ~20% funded in April.
UN State Dept FAO · famine

Bengal tiger · India, 2022 census

3,682

wild tigers, up from about 1,400 in 2006 — roughly 75% of every wild tiger alive

How they know
Camera traps and DNA from scat across 20 states. Largest wildlife survey on the planet.
The catch
Gains sit inside reserves. The corridors between them are highways and farmland now, and the Sundarbans habitat, in Bangladesh, is going under with sea level rise.
Why it's here
Because it went up. Keep reading.
Mongabay Corridors 2026

Tropical forest · 2025 satellite data

4.3M ha

primary rainforest lost in one year — eleven football pitches a minute

The situation
Down 36% from 2024's record, mostly because there were fewer fires. Still 46% above a decade ago.
Pressure works
Brazil cut non-fire forest loss 41% in a single year, to the lowest on record, after enforcement changed. Somebody made cutting it expensive.
Who's counting
Maryland's GLAD lab, from satellites, published openly.
WRI Forest Watch

Air & plastic · annual

~7M

premature deaths a year linked to air pollution - WHO. Estimates run 4–8M by method, aka the number is "soft".

Not far away
April 2026: 152.3 million Americans (44%) live in a county that failed at least one air quality standard. Including 33.5 million people under 18.
Plastic
Over 450 million tonnes made a year. Under 10% of all plastic ever made has been recycled.
Why it's here
Not a "far-away people's problem." Check your ZIP.

What winning looks like.

1972: ~1,800 wild tigers left & falling. 1973: Project Tiger. Reserves, wardens, money. 50 years later it has doubled+.

Not luck or charity but constant/boring pressure, applied by people publicly mocked for caring.
