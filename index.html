Fund

Give where it lands.

Rising Tigers doesn’t handle your money. Each link goes straight to the organization, and the article explains why we trust these three.

  • Congo

    Panzi Foundation

    Trauma surgery, mental health care, legal aid, job training. Congolese-run; founded by 2018 Nobel Peace laureate Dr. Mukwege.

    Donate
  • Palestine

    UNRWA Gaza Appeal

    Cash aid, food, shelter, healthcare, and education for refugees. A UN agency mandated since 1949; about 11% overhead, the rest direct aid.

    Donate
  • Sudan

    Mutual Aid Sudan

    Communal kitchens, medical care, evacuations, water repairs. $0.95 of every $1 reaches responders within two weeks.

    Donate

Also on the article’s list: Doctors Without Borders, International Rescue Committee, Norwegian Refugee Council, UNHCR, and World Central Kitchen.

Want to check any charity yourself? Charity Navigator, Candid, and CharityWatch publish their methods.

Can’t give right now? Each place has a hub of free ways to help: Congo, Sudan, and Palestine.

San Francisco Bay Area

In danger or in crisis

  • 911
    Emergency Police, fire, medical.
  • 988
    Suicide & Crisis Lifeline Call or text 988, or chat online. Press 2 or text AYUDA for Spanish. Free, 24/7. 988lifeline.org
  • 741741
    Crisis Text Line Text HOME. Free, 24/7. crisistextline.org
  • 877-503-1850
    La Casa de las Madres San Francisco domestic violence hotline, 24/7. Teens: 877-923-0700. lacasa.org

More national lines, including sexual assault, trafficking, and LGBTQ+ youth, are on the United States tab.

Immigration and legal help

  • SF Rapid Response Hotline
    If someone was detained by immigration enforcement in San Francisco today. Community-run, often one person answering, so call back if busy. 415-200-1548
  • SF Immigrant Support Hub
    Find free or low-cost immigration legal help by language and case type.
  • Dolores Street Community Services
    Immigration legal services in English, Spanish, and Hindi. 415-282-6209
  • Asian Law Caucus
    Immigration legal services for low-income Asian Pacific Islander communities. 415-896-1701
  • Legal Services for Children
    Free immigration legal services for children and youth under 21. 415-863-3762
  • Pangea Legal Services
    Immigration legal services and deportation defense. 415-254-0475

Only a licensed attorney or a Department of Justice-accredited representative can give immigration legal advice. A notary public (or “notario”) cannot. To find free or low-cost nonprofit help anywhere in the US, search the Immigration Advocates Network directory.

Support for your community

  • Maitri
    South Asian survivors of domestic violence, trafficking, and family conflict. Weekday helpline in many South Asian languages. DOJ-accredited to help survivors with immigration. 1-888-862-4874
  • Narika
    South Asian women facing domestic violence. Helpline, safety planning, and legal and housing referrals. 1-800-215-7308

Art and culture

  • SF Cultural Centers
    African American, American Indian, Asian Pacific Islander, Latino, and Queer and Trans arts centers, plus Bayview Opera House and SOMArts. Free or low-cost shows and classes.
  • Kearny Street Workshop
    Asian Pacific American arts since 1972: exhibitions, readings, and artist fellowships.
  • Museum of the African Diaspora
    Contemporary art by artists of the African diaspora, at 685 Mission Street.
  • Pt. 2 Gallery, Oakland
    Contemporary art centering Black culture and social justice.

Artists to look up. Mildred Howard, a mixed-media artist born in San Francisco and raised in Berkeley (Academy of Art). Enrique Chagoya, whose artist’s books set Mesoamerican codex traditions against political collage (Latina). Muzae Sesay and Lenworth “Joonbug” McIntosh, Oakland artists (7x7).

Europe

In danger or in crisis

  • 112
    Emergency Police, medical, fire. Free from any phone in every EU country. traficom.fi
  • 116 123
    Emotional support Listening for loneliness, crisis, or thoughts of suicide. Not active in every country. traficom.fi
  • 116 111
    Child helpline Not active in every country. traficom.fi
  • 116 000
    Missing children Not active in every country. traficom.fi
  • 116 006
    Victims of crime Emotional support and information about your rights. Not active in every country. traficom.fi

A 116 number may not be active where you are. If it doesn’t connect, call 112. Ireland: text 50808 to Crisis Text Line (crisistextline.org). The UK isn’t in the EU: see the United Kingdom tab.

About

The Rising Tigers Initiative makes hard-to-access information usable. We mentor ambitious people (“Rising Tigers”) through college applications, careers, and immigration, supported by a network of volunteer professionals (“Luminaries”) across North America and Europe. We also publish independent, sourced research on underreported humanitarian and environmental issues.

Sourced
Every number links to its reference and the date we checked it. If something is wrong, the repository records the fix and your name.
Free and open
No ads. Code is MIT-licensed; writing and data are CC BY 4.0.
Direct
Tigers don’t mess around, so neither do we: plain numbers, named sources, no euphemism.
