## Bengal tiger

### tiger-census-2022
**Claim:** India's 2022 tiger census counted 3,682 wild tigers (range 3,167–3,925), roughly 75% of all wild tigers worldwide, up from about 1,400 in 2006.
**Figure type:** range
**Value:** 3,682 (3,167–3,925)
**Source:** NTCA, via Mongabay — https://india.mongabay.com/2025/02/the-rise-fall-and-return-of-indias-tigers/
**Checked:** 2026-08-18

### tiger-series
**Claim:** NTCA's mean census estimates by cycle: 1,411 (2006) → 1,706 (2010) → 2,226 (2014) → 2,967 (2018) → 3,682 (2022).
**Figure type:** count
**Value:** see series above
**Source:** NTCA mean estimates
**Checked:** 2026-08-18
**Caveat:** These are means; uncertainty bands exist around each figure. Method: camera traps plus scat DNA across 20 states.

**Claim:** Corridor fragmentation and Sundarbans habitat loss to sea-level rise threaten continued recovery.
**Figure type:** count
**Value:** —

**Checked:** 2026-08-18

### tiger-project-tiger-origin
